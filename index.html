CALI ENGINE / v1.0 / TACTICAL DATA SENSITIVITY

01

DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Your training data is your tactical advantage. The Cali Engine is engineered as a local-first system. Every rep, set, and biometric data point is stored exclusively within your device's protected sandbox. We do not harvest, monetize, or transmit your personal intelligence to external servers.

02

LOCAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Persistence is handled through high-performance local indexing. This allows for full functionality without an active uplink. Your progress is tied to your hardware identity—meaning if you lose your device, your local data remains secure and inaccessible to third parties.

03

ANALYTICAL TELEMETRY

We may utilize anonymized diagnostic logs solely for the purpose of engine optimization. This telemetry identifies technical failures and performance bottlenecks but never captures user-specific training routines, biometric markers, or physiological data.
